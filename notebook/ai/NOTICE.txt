Melody AI for aQWERTYon

Inspired by A.I. Duet (2016), by Yotam Mann with Google Creative Lab and Magenta.
https://yotammann.info/aiexperiments
https://github.com/googlecreativelab/aiexperiments-ai-duet
This is a new integration, not a restoration of that server or a Google product.

Uses @magenta/music 1.23.1, copyright Google, Apache License 2.0.
Uses TensorFlow.js 2.7.0, copyright Google, Apache License 2.0.
Complete licenses accompany the runtime in vendor/.
Magenta's hosted basic_rnn checkpoint is mirrored locally as permitted by its
checkpoint documentation: https://github.com/magenta/magenta-js/blob/master/music/checkpoints/README.md
Exact sources, sizes, SHA-256 values: provenance.json.

Model inference runs in a Web Worker on this device. No notes are sent to a
model server. Model/runtime downloads occur only on Start AI. Existing
instrument soundfonts retain their original hosting.

Musical adaptation: monophonic upper voice from chords; prompt octave
normalization; phrase-derived tempo; moderate model temperature; reply
register/scale projection and bounded leaps; the player's onset spacing and
articulation; a related opening motif and optional tonic/fifth ending in
Related mode. Explore uses higher sampling temperature and no forced ending.
These are design choices, not a claim of objectively superior musicality.
